Kechimochi Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-23

This Privacy Policy applies to Kechimochi, developed by Federico (Morg) Pareschi.

Data Collection

Kechimochi does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information or user data with the developer.

The app does not use:

  • User tracking
  • Analytics or usage metrics
  • Advertising tracking
  • User profiling
  • Behavioral monitoring
  • Telemetry
  • Personally identifiable information collection
  • Any services or tools that connect any sort of telemetry to the developer

User-Provided Data

Any data or information entered, created, imported, or otherwise provided by the user within Kechimochi remains under the user's full control.

The developers of Kechimochi do not retrieve, access, collect, transmit, or receive this information.

Where applicable, user data is stored locally on the user's device and is not sent to the developers or to any developer-operated server.

Third-Party Sharing

Kechimochi does not sell, share, or provide user data to third parties.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the functionality of Kechimochi changes. Any changes will be published on this page.
